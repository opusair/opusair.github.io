The Log is the Agent

2026-07-05 02:57:38

A research paper titled 'The Log is the Agent' proposes a new paradigm in the design of artificial intelligence agents by treating an agent's execution history, or log, as the central driver of its actions and decision-making. Instead of relying on external, static control structures, this method uses the operation log as a dynamic state container. This architectural shift enables autonomous agents to achieve continuous learning, self-reference, and more adaptive planning in dynamic environments. The paper details how logging mechanisms can be elevated from passive diagnostics to active computational drivers in large language models. (source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21997)
