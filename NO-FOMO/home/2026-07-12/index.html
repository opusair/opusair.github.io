The One-Step Trap (In AI Research)

2026-07-12 18:41:15

AI researchers have identified the One-Step Trap, a persistent cognitive bias where algorithms are designed and evaluated based on single-step predictions rather than multi-step sequential interactions. This theoretical limitation leads to models that perform well in static environments but fail to generalize in dynamic, closed-loop systems. By focusing on immediate outputs, researchers bypass the challenges of cumulative error propagation, temporal credit assignment, and long-term planning. To address this, the article argues that the AI community must shift toward reinforcement learning frameworks and multi-step dynamical systems that account for sequential dependencies. (source: http://incompleteideas.net/IncIdeas/OneStepTrap.html)
